Smart Court – Product Feature Specification
1. Product Overview
Smart Court is an AI-powered legal marketplace designed to connect clients with the most suitable lawyers in Egypt. The platform combines artificial intelligence, lawyer matching, contract management, secure communication, and escrow payments into a single web application.

Unlike traditional legal marketplaces, Smart Court assists both clients and lawyers through specialized AI systems. Clients receive guidance to improve their legal cases before hiring a lawyer, while lawyers receive an AI assistant that supports legal research and case analysis.

The initial release targets the Egyptian legal system and supports the Arabic language only.
2. User Roles
2.1 Client
A client is an individual seeking legal assistance.
•	Register and manage an account.
•	Use the AI Legal Assistant.
•	Create and manage legal cases.
•	Receive AI analysis for submitted cases.
•	Improve and resubmit cases multiple times.
•	Submit finalized cases for lawyer matching.
•	Browse lawyer profiles.
•	Send proposals to lawyers.
•	Negotiate through chat.
•	Sign contracts.
•	Upload documents and evidence.
•	Track active contracts.
•	Review lawyers after project completion.
•	Receive platform notifications.
2.2 Lawyer
A lawyer is a verified legal professional.
•	Register for an account.
•	Submit verification documents.
•	Create and manage a professional profile.
•	Receive proposals from clients.
•	Communicate with clients.
•	Negotiate contract terms.
•	Sign contracts.
•	Manage active legal projects.
•	Use the Lawyer AI Assistant.
•	Publish legal articles.
•	Review clients.
•	Receive notifications.
2.3 Administrator
Administrators manage the platform.
•	Verify lawyers.
•	Manage user accounts.
•	Suspend accounts.
•	Moderate articles.
•	Handle disputes.
•	View system statistics.
•	Monitor platform activity.
3. Authentication Module
The platform provides:
- Client registration.
- Lawyer registration.
- Secure login.
- Email verification.
- Password reset.
- Profile management.
4. Lawyer Verification Module
Lawyers must complete identity verification before using the marketplace.

Verification requires National ID and Egyptian Bar Association membership number. Verification requests are reviewed by administrators. Only verified lawyers may receive proposals or publish articles.
5. AI Legal Assistant
Answers common legal questions, explains procedures and terminology, and provides general legal guidance. The AI always displays a disclaimer indicating that its responses do not constitute legal advice.
6. Legal Case Management
Clients create cases containing a title, legal category, detailed description, and supporting documents (PDF, images, audio, or video). Clients may save drafts, edit cases, and submit them when ready.
7. AI Case Analysis
The AI identifies strength points, weak points, missing information, and suggested improvements. Clients may repeatedly improve and resubmit cases before final submission.
8. Lawyer Matching Engine
The system recommends lawyers based on legal specialization, years of experience, case complexity, lawyer availability, and location. The system never automatically assigns a lawyer; the client makes the final decision.
9. Lawyer Marketplace
Clients may browse, search, and filter lawyers, view profiles, specializations, experience, published articles, and reviews.
10. Proposal Management
Clients initiate collaboration by sending proposals. Lawyers may accept or reject proposals. Accepting a proposal opens a dedicated communication channel.
11. Communication Module
Supports text messages, file attachments, and voice messages in a private workspace.
12. Contract Management
Supports fixed-price, hourly, and milestone contracts including scope of work, deliverables, milestones, budget, hourly rate, dates, and additional terms. Contracts become active only after both parties approve them.
13. Escrow Payment System
Client deposits funds, the platform reserves them, the lawyer completes the work, the client approves completion, and funds are released. If a dispute occurs, administrators review the case.
14. Lawyer AI Assistant
Provides legal research, case summarization, contract drafting assistance, and Egyptian law retrieval through a RAG system.
15. Article Publishing System
Verified lawyers may publish categorized legal articles immediately. Featured article placement is supported.
16. Review and Rating System
Clients may review lawyers and lawyers may review clients after contract completion.
17. Notification System
Supports in-app, email, and SMS notifications.
18. Privacy and Security
Clients may access only their own legal cases. Lawyers may access only cases associated with proposals sent to them. Sensitive information is protected through authentication, authorization, encryption, and secure communication. All AI responses include a disclaimer that they do not replace professional legal advice.
19. Platform Constraints
Initial release supports Egyptian law only, Arabic language only, and web application only. Mobile apps, audio/video calls, automatic lawyer assignment, and multi-country legal systems are outside the MVP scope.
20. Future Enhancements
Android and iOS applications, video consultations, voice calls, advanced AI capabilities, subscription plans, expanded legal jurisdictions, additional languages, and enhanced analytics.
